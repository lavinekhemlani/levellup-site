Questions

before you enrol.

What's the difference between a Mini and a Short Course?

A Mini is one live 2-hour session: the fastest way to try a track and leave with something useful. The Short Course is the full live cohort: four weeks for the AI tracks, three weeks and a 12-hour certificate for Cyber, ending with something shipped.

How much does it cost?

Every track is priced the same. Mini: US$99 early bird (regular US$150). Short Course: US$1,000 early bird (regular US$1,500). Prices are also shown in HKD, SGD, AED, GBP, CAD and PHP.

When does the next cohort start?

Tuesday 1 December 2026, for all three Short Courses. Mini sessions run from December and we confirm your date by email when you enrol.

How long does the early-bird price last?

Early-bird pricing applies to the 1 December cohort while seats last. Your refund rights are the same either way.

Which track should I pick?

AI for Founders if you want to sell an AI offer or run your company leaner. AI for Automation if you want to win back hours in your current job. Cyber if you want a new, well-paid security skill and paid gigs after. Still unsure? Compare all tracks on the courses page or message Aka on WhatsApp.

Is this actually live, or am I watching recordings?

Every session runs live with a real instructor. Replays exist so you never lose a session you miss.

Can I try it before paying?

Yes. Join a free 90-minute live workshop, or start with a Mini for one session.

What's the refund policy?

Full refund up to the halfway point of your Short Course, no questions asked. After that, the outcome guarantee applies: do the work and don't ship your track's deliverable by the final session, and you get your money back. See the refund policy.

Do I need paid tools?

You can do most of the course on free tiers. Some builds go further with a paid Claude or ChatGPT plan (about US$20 a month) or a paid automation plan. We tell you before each session what you need, and never require a tool we earn commission on.

Can I pay by FPS, PayMe or bank transfer?

Yes. Choose "Prefer bank transfer or FPS? Send me a payment link" at checkout and we send you FPS or bank transfer details for Hong Kong, or a secure card link anywhere else. Your seat is held for 7 days while you pay.

Can my employer pay?

Yes. Use the employer email template, and we can issue an invoice addressed to your company.

Is this subsidised or free through a government programme?

No. LevelUp is paid, independently run and founder-led. That is how we keep cohorts small and live.

I'm not technical. Can I still do this?

Yes. Every track is built for founders, operators and career switchers, not engineers. No coding background required.

What is LevelUp Jobs?

Paid, hourly gigs for graduates, from AI automation builds to security testing. See the Jobs page.
